### Overall Evaluation

**1. Working Environment**

The learning and internship environment was friendly and professional. Members of the program were always willing to provide support whenever difficulties arose and encouraged interns to take initiative in learning. The atmosphere was comfortable while still maintaining professionalism and productivity.

**2. Mentor / Admin Team Support**

The mentors and admin team were always enthusiastic in answering questions throughout the learning process and project implementation. The learning materials were well prepared, the roadmap was clearly organized, and technical or AWS account issues were supported promptly whenever needed.

**3. Relevance to My Academic Major**

The internship content was highly relevant to my Information Technology major, especially in the fields of Cloud Computing and Artificial Intelligence. The hands-on labs and project allowed me to apply the knowledge I learned at university while exploring many AWS services in real-world scenarios.

**4. Learning & Skill Development Opportunities**

The program helped me strengthen my knowledge of AWS, Serverless Architecture, AI Services, Cloud Security, and cost optimization. In addition to technical knowledge, I also improved my self-learning ability, technical research skills, teamwork, and technical report writing.

**5. Culture & Teamwork**

Everyone was willing to support and share their experience throughout the learning process. Team members collaborated effectively and worked together to solve issues encountered during labs and project development.

---

### Additional Questions

**What was your favorite part of the internship?**

The most valuable experience was having the opportunity to work with a wide range of AWS services through practical labs and to build a complete Serverless project. The structured learning roadmap helped me understand how to design and deploy cloud-based systems on AWS.

**What do you think should be improved for future interns?**

The program could include more experience-sharing sessions or advanced workshops on newly released AWS services. In addition, some labs required AWS services that were unavailable on educational accounts. Providing alternative solutions or detailed guidance in such cases would improve the learning experience.

**Would you recommend this program to your friends? Why?**

Yes. I would recommend this program to students who are interested in AWS and Cloud Computing. The curriculum is practical, includes many hands-on labs, and provides an excellent opportunity to improve both technical knowledge and cloud implementation skills.

### Suggestions & Future Expectations

- Organize more AWS events, workshops, and community activities so that participants can network, exchange knowledge, and stay updated with the latest AWS technologies.
- Continue expanding the hands-on labs with more real-world project scenarios and the latest AWS AI services.
- Provide additional experience-sharing sessions from mentors and Cloud Engineers working in the industry.
- If possible, increase the number of internship slots available for on-site participation at the office, allowing more students to experience a professional working environment and interact directly with mentors and the AWS community.
- I hope to continue participating in future AWS First Cloud AI Journey programs and activities to further improve my cloud knowledge and technical skills.
